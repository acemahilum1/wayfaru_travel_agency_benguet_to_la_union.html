4-Day Adventure

Comprehensive Tour Itinerary

From the misty heights of Benguet to the sun-kissed beaches of La Union.

Day 1 • Stop 1

Valley of Colors (Stobosa)

Walk across the scenic suspension bridge for amazing views and taste famous creamy strawberry taho — a Baguio must-try!

  • Scenic suspension bridge walk
  • Famous strawberry taho tasting
Day 1 • Stop 2

Tam-awan Village

Immerse in rich Cordilleran culture, art, and stunning mountain scenery. Enjoy optional portrait sketching to take home your own art keepsake.

  • Rich Cordilleran art & traditional huts
  • Optional portrait sketching keepsake
Day 1 • Stop 3

Mines View Observation Deck

Breathtaking panoramic views of Cordillera mountains and valleys. Fun optional traditional costume rental for photos.

  • Panoramic Cordillera mountain views
  • Optional traditional costume rentals
Day 1 • Stop 4

Wright Park and The Mansion

Lush pine trees, peaceful gardens, and iconic royal-style gate. Optional horseback riding fun for all ages.

  • Scenic pine tree walks & royal gate
  • Optional horseback riding adventures
Day 1 • Stop 5

Baguio Night Market

Lively street food scene — taste local favorites, great ukay-ukay finds & affordable handmade souvenirs. Best way to end the day with food, fun & memories.

  • Authentic local street food tasting
  • Ukay-ukay shopping and souvenirs
Day 2 • Stop 1

La Trinidad Strawberry Farm

Pick your own fresh strawberries straight from the fields! Perfect for family fun and fresh pasalubong.

  • Fresh strawberry picking experience
  • Local pasalubong shopping
Day 2 • Stop 2

Igorot Village & Nature Trails

Deep dive into authentic Cordilleran traditions & architecture. Learn about local tribes and their rich history, plus peaceful walks along Pine Forest Yellow Trail and Mt. Tayawan Agri-Tourist Park.

  • Cultural immersion & tribal architecture
  • Pine forest yellow trail & agri-tourist park
Day 2 • Stop 3

Pine Forest(yellow Trail)

Peaceful, easy walking trails — great for relaxing & unwinding

  • Nature's own stress reliever — breathe, slow down, recharge
  • Suitable for all ages & pet-friendly too!
Day 2 • Stop 4

Mt. Tayawan Agri-Tourist Park

Peaceful, easy walking trails — great for relaxing & unwinding

  • Nature's own stress reliever — breathe, slow down, recharge
  • Suitable for all ages & pet-friendly too!
Day 3 • Stop 1

Ma-cho Temple

Philippines' first and only 7-tiered Taoist temple — truly one-of-a-kind. Golden roofs, peaceful gardens, shared heritage. Everyone's welcome! Dedicated to Mazu, Goddess of Travelers, to bless your journey.

  • 7-tiered magnificent Taoist temple architecture
  • Peaceful gardens & panoramic sea views
Day 3 • Stop 2

Gapuz Grapes Farm

Boodle fight under vines, unique dining experience among the vineyards. Grape juice, wine & local products; great pasalubong too! Harvest fresh grapes yourself.

  • Vineyard dining & grape harvesting
  • Local wine & fresh grape tasting
Day 3 • Stop 3

San Juan Beach

Surfing Capital of the North! Waves for every level. Golden shores to swim, relax, play, or just lounge. Easy escape from Manila for pure coastal joy.

  • World-class surfing & golden sand shores
  • Sunset lounging & coastal vibe
Day 3 • Stop 4

Pindangan Ruins

The oldest Spanish-era stone church in La Union. A born-when-two-coastal-communities-joined landmark with peaceful sanctuary, history, and calm timeless beauty.

  • Historic Spanish-era stone architecture
  • Serene and timeless sanctuary grounds
Day 4 • Stop 1

Baluarte Watchtower

Coastal guardian and original lookout against pirates & invaders; served as WWII communication post too. The symbol of Luna's resilience with pebble beach perch and stunning sunset & photo spot.

  • Historic coastal watchtower structure
  • Pebble beach perch & sunset viewpoint
Day 4 • Stop 2

Bahay na Bato

Open-air art gallery — whimsical stone & wood sculptures throughout the grounds, designed by artist Bong Kim. A 2-storey museum from antique displays, pebble-mosaic flooring, and a stunning panoramic deck overlooking the sea.

  • Open-air art gallery & stone sculptures
  • 2-storey museum overlooking the sea
